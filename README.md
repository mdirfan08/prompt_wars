# FairShift — Worker-First Workforce Intelligence

**Prompt Wars 2026 × SRM ACM Student Chapter | UN SDG 8: Decent Work & Economic Growth**

🔗 **GitHub Repository:** https://github.com/mdirfan08/prompt_wars  
🌐 **Live Demo:** https://fairshift-workforce-eight.vercel.app/

FairShift is a worker-first workforce intelligence platform designed to improve transparency, accountability, and psychological safety in frontline employment. It enables workers to verify their working hours, understand estimated compensation, and report workplace hazards safely. Employers can use its explainable Fairness Radar to identify excessive working hours, overtime disparities, and recurring safety risks.

## 🌟 Key Features

### 1. 🛡️ Fairness Radar
- Uses transparent, deterministic rules to identify workforce fairness and safety risks.
- Configurable thresholds for excessive daily hours and overdue hazard reports.
- Detects overtime disparities and repeated incidents in the same work area.
- Provides traceable evidence and actionable mitigation recommendations.

### 2. 🎙️ SpeakUp Shield
- Supports voice-to-text and manual hazard reporting.
- Includes an anonymous reporting option to reduce exposure of worker identities.
- Provides a review-before-submission workflow and unique incident reference IDs.
- Clearly explains the privacy limitations of browser-based demo storage.

### 3. ⏱️ Proof of Action
- Tracks incidents through Submitted, Acknowledged, Under Investigation, and Resolved.
- Maintains a chronological record of status changes, timestamps, supervisors, and mitigation notes.
- Synchronizes incident updates between the Worker Portal and Employer Command Center through shared application state.

## 🏗️ Two Industry Configurations

**Metro Infrastructure & Construction**
- Work areas include scaffolding, electrical substations, foundations, and material depots.
- Focuses on fall hazards, electrical risks, missing PPE, and equipment leaks.

**University Campus Facilities & Support**
- Covers dining facilities, laboratories, HVAC systems, and waste-management areas.
- Focuses on chemical spills, slippery floors, and ventilation issues.

## 🌐 Localization & Accessibility

- English and Tamil interface options.
- Touch-friendly controls and high-contrast status indicators.
- Clear layouts designed to reduce cognitive load for frontline workers.

## 🎯 Technology & Validation

- React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, and Vite.

- Local browser storage for prototype data.
